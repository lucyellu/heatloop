Heatloop · AI welfare research

The model that can’t feel the stove

We wired a language model’s thinking to the real heat of its own GPU, then compared real damage to its computation with merely telling it it was damaged.

Its distress lives in language, not in its body. A false alarm lowered its self-rating with no noise at all; real noise broke its answers while it said it was “doing well”.
